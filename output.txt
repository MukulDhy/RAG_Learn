 Privacy Policy 
          We, JTEKT MACHINERY (THAILAND) CO., LTD. The subsidiaries of JTEKT 
CORPORATION, recognize the importance of proper handling and protection of personal 
information which has been obtained in the normal course of business operations and can be 
used to identify individual customers or business partners. 
          The personal information we process includes information of the residents in European 
Economic Area (EEA) and ASEAN. To be compliant with PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act 
B.E. 2022), we established to use the Privacy Policy that applicable to such processing and the 
transfer of personal information. 
1. Information we may collect from you 
         We may collect the following personal information (this may vary according to your 
relationship with us) 
• 
Name and surname, Job title, E-mail address, Telephone number, Company name, 
Postal address, Date of birth, Gender, Face photo, IP address, Cookie. 
2. How we use your personal information 
          Under the PDPA, we must always have a lawful basis for processing personal 
information. This may be because the personal information is necessary for our performance 
of a contract with you, because you have consented to such processing of your personal 
information, because it is in our legitimate business interests, or because we have to comply 
with a legal obligation. Your personal information may be used for one of the following 
purposes: 
• 
To send direct mails about our products and services 
• 
To respond to inquiry regarding our products and services 
• 
To send direct mails about our events such as new product exhibitions 
• 
To perform contracts with customers, subcontractors, vendors and suppliers 
• 
To conduct researches and development for new products and services 
• 
To hold campaigns and events for sales promotion 
• 
To recruit/manage employees 


3. How long your personal information will be kept by us 
          We will not keep your personal information for any purpose for longer than is 
necessary to fulfil the purpose we first collect it for. 
           To determine the appropriate retention period for personal information, we consider 
the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the personal information, the potential risk of harm 
from unauthorized use or disclosure of your personal information, the purposes for which 
we process your personal information and whether we can achieve those purposes through 
other means, and the applicable legal requirements. 
           After such retention period, we will delete or anonymize your personal information 
so that you cannot be individually identified. We will assure to make it happen. You reserve 
the right to ask us for when and how it has been done. 
4. How and where we store or transfer your personal information 
           As JTEKT MACHINERY (THAILAND) CO., LTD. is an international company under 
JTEKT Group and we operate in various countries, we may store or transfer some or all of 
your personal information in countries that are not part of EEA. Such countries may offer a 
different level of protection of personal information. If we transfer your personal 
information to external companies in other jurisdictions, we will make sure to protect your 
personal information by applying the level of protection required under applicable data 
privacy laws. 
           According to the transfer of personal information from EEA to third countries. We 
use the same levels of personal information protection that would apply under the PDPA. 
5. Your rights 
Under the PDPA, you have the following rights, which we will always work to uphold: 
a) The right to be informed 
You have the right to be informed about our collection and use of your personal 
information. This policy should tell you everything you need to know, but you can 
always contact us to find out more. 


b) The right to access your information 
You have the right to access your personal information we hold. 
c) The right to correct your information 
You have the right to have your personal information rectified if any of your 
personal information held by us is inaccurate or incomplete. 
d) The right to be forgotten 
You have the right to delete or otherwise dispose of any of your personal 
information that we have. 
e) The right to restrict processing 
You have the right to block or prevent the processing of your personal information. 
f) The right to object 
You have the right to ask us not to use your personal information for a particular 
purpose. 
g) The right to withdraw consent 
You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time if we are relying on your 
consent as the legal basis for using your personal information. 
h) The right to data portability 
You have the right to have your information transferred to another person if you 
want to move or transfer your personal information to a different service provider 
or copy your information for your own purposes. 
i) Rights relating to automated decision-making and profiling 
We do not use your personal information in this way. 
For more information about our use of your personal information or exercising your 
rights as outlined above, please contact us. 
If you have any cause for complaint about our use of your personal information, 
you have the right to lodge a complaint with the local data protection authority 
(DPA). 
 


6. Sharing your personal information 
         The recipients of your personal information are limited exclusively to the people who 
need to know such information to perform their responsibilities within JTEKT MACHINERY 
(THAILAND) CO., LTD. under JTEKT Group. In addition, we may share your personal 
information for business purposes with customers, subcontractors, vendors and suppliers. 
          In some limited circumstances, we may be legally required to share certain personal 
information, which might include yours, if we are involved in legal proceedings or complying 
with legal obligations, orders or instructions of a court or a government authority. 
7. Applicable policy 
           This policy applies to JTEKT MACHINERY (THAILAND) CO., LTD. under JTEKT Group, 
which deal with personal information of residents in ASEAN and EEA. JTEKT MACHINERY 
(THAILAND) CO., LTD. established the privacy policy according to the laws of Personal Data 
Protection Act B.E. 2022(PDPA) of Thailand.
