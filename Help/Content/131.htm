Instructions Toolbar

Topic: CL131

Description

The dockable Instruction Toolbar provides one-click access to the more commonly used ContactLadder Instructions. Place the Box Cursor anywhere in the Contact Area of the Ladder Editor and click on the desired Instruction. The Instruction Dialog will open.

The Instruction Toolbar shown above gives quick access to the following Instructions:

  1. Normally Open Contact
  2. Normally Closed Contact
  3. Normally Open Immediate Contact
  4. Normally Closed Immediate Contact
  5. Positive Differential Contact /Rising Edge Contact
  6. Negative Differential Contact /Falling Edge Contact
  7. Compare Functions

Toggle the Edit Toolbar On or Off

From View Menu > Toolbar, toggle any of the dockable One-click Toolbars Off or On.
