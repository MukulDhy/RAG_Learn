Calendar/Clock Setup

Topic: CL138

Description

This dialog allows you to check the current Date and Time of the CLICK PLC and change them if necessary. The Standard and Analog CPU modules support this feature.

Setup

1 Date & Time of PLC: Displays the current Date and Time of the CLICK PLC.

2Setting: When you want to update the Date and Time in the CLICK PLC, there are two options:

  1. Adjust to PC Clock:Copy the current Date and Time of the PC to the CLICK PLC.
  2. Manual: You can enter the Date and Time in the following Written Date and Time fields.

3Written Date and Time: This option is available when you select the Manual setting. Enter the Date in the Date field or click the down arrow button to the right of the field and select a date from the calendar that opens as shown below.  Enter the Time in the Time field or click the up and down arrows to the right of the field to adjust the Time as needed.

4Write to PLC: Click this button to update the Date and Time in the CLICK PLC with the settings you selected.
