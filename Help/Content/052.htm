Topic: CL052

Effect of Zoom Out

Select Zoom Out from the View Menu (see below) or by clicking the Zoom Out button on the Toolbar. Zoom Out causes the Ladder Editor to shrink within the boundaries of the space available.

Select Zoom In to magnify the Ladder Editor incrementally or Zoom 100% to return to normal viewing in one step.

Selecting Zoom Out from the View Menu

Select Zoom In from the View Menu.
