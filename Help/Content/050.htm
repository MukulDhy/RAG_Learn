Topic: CL050

Toggle the Coil Area Off or On

The Coil Area of the Ladder Editor can easily be turned on to see the Coil Area devices or off to see more of the enabling rung logic.

The Coil Area (column AF) can be opened or closed using the View Menu.

The Coil Area can also be toggled on or off using the Show/Hide button labeled Coil Area.
