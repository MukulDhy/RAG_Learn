CLICK Project Loader

Topic: CL159

Description

The CLICK Project Loader is used to open a CLICK Loader File and write the CLICK Project to the CLICK PLC with or without the Password protection. The CLICK Project Loader also has the following features:

  1. Make a Backup Copy of the CLICK Project and Firmware in the CLICK PLC.
