Time/Date Compare

Topic: CL252

 

This help topic illustrates how to create ladder logic to determine Time and Date comparisons. The System Data Registers SD19 through SD26 contain the Real Time Clock values of: Year, Month, Day, Day-of-Week, Hour, Minute, Second. These registers can be used in Compare Contacts, and several contacts can be combined in logic to decide when Time and Date events have occurred.

Business Day Example

This example output (C4), which is ON during normal business hours, Monday to Friday, between 8:30AM and 5:30PM.

This example converts hours (SD24) and minutes (SD25) to seconds and adds this to the seconds (SD26). Then it similarly calculates the seconds of the day to turn on and the seconds of the day to turn off and compares the present PLC time in seconds to these values.
