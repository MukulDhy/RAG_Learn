Zoom 100 %

Topic: CL053

Restore Normal Viewing

After applying the Zoom In or Zoom Out feature, the Zoom 100% feature becomes active. Until then, it is grayed out on the View Menu and the Toolbar. Select the Zoom 100% feature to restore the Ladder Editor to normal viewing after zooming in or out.

Select Zoom 100% from Menu

Select Zoom 100% from the View Menu.
