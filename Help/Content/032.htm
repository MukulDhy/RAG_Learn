Redo

Topic: CL032

Description

The Redo Icon is located on the Edit Toolbar. The Redo Selection is available from theEdit Menu, located on the Main Menu.

The Redo selection allows the user to repeat one step or more after Undoing a Cut or Delete. Redo must be used immediately after the Undo. For example, if you Cut a rung element and then Undothe Cut to restore the original version, but you decide the Cut was appropriate, click on Redo.

Note: The Undo and Redo functions save up to 20 levels of Undos and Redos.
