Paste

Topic: CL035

Description

The Paste Icon is located on the Edit Toolbar and the Paste Menu selection is available from the Edit Menu, located on the Main Menu.

The Paste selection is used to Paste or Place a Copied or Cut item from the clipboard into the project. When the Paste function is selected, an object or text is placed on the work area at the selected location.  

To Paste an item, click on the work area and then click on either of the Paste selections. You can also use the Ctrl+V shortcut to Paste and item.
